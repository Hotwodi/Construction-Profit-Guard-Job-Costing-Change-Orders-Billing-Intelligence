Overview

Construction Profit Guard is an AI-powered Odoo module designed for construction contractors and project managers. It provides end-to-end job costing, change order management, AIA-style progress billing, subcontractor compliance tracking, and intelligent margin forecasting — all in one integrated application.

Key Features

Project Management

Track construction projects with contract values, budgets, committed and incurred costs, completion percentages, and AI margin forecasts.

Cost Code Management

Organize costs by type (labor, material, equipment, subcontractor, overhead) with budget vs. actual variance and AI overrun risk scoring.

Job Cost Entries

Record costs from purchase orders, timesheets, vendor bills, inventory, or manual entries with AI auto-categorization.

Change Orders

Manage the full change order lifecycle from request to invoice, with customer approval tracking and AI detection from daily logs.

Daily Site Logs

Capture weather, labor count, equipment, deliveries, blockers, and safety notes with photo attachments and AI change-order flagging.

Progress Billing (AIA)

Generate AIA-style progress billings with schedule of values, retention calculations, and multi-state approval workflow.

Subcontractor Compliance

Monitor insurance policies, COI expiry dates, and compliance scores with automatic status alerts for expiring or expired documents.

Commitment Tracking

Track purchase orders, subcontracts, and inventory transfers with committed vs. incurred amounts and remaining balances.

AI Intelligence

The module includes AI-powered features across all core areas: margin forecasting based on completion trajectory, budget overrun risk scoring per cost code, automatic job cost entry categorization, and change order detection from daily site log analysis.
